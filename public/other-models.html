Mistral (France)

Strengths: practical open and commercial models with strong price-performance.

  • Mistral Large 3 — Flagship frontier line in Mistral's current studio lineup. Public model pages confirm positioning, but this pass did not cleanly extract a simple first-party token-price table
  • Ministral 3 family — Smaller efficient family for lighter-weight deployments. Current vendor pages confirm the family, but exact token pricing should be rechecked live before quoting

Sources: Mistral models · Amazon Bedrock pricing

Cohere (Canada)

Strengths: enterprise and retrieval-oriented deployment patterns.

  • Command — Enterprise-focused text model line. $1.00 / $2.00
  • Command R+ 08-2024 — Higher-capability retrieval-oriented tier still listed on Cohere's pricing page. $2.50 / $10.00

Sources: OpenRouter model page · Cohere pricing

AI21 (Israel)

Strengths: long-context architectures and enterprise deployment focus.

  • Jamba family — AI21 publicly lists Jamba Mini and Jamba Large. Rendered pricing now exposes token rates: Jamba Mini $0.2 / $0.4; Jamba Large $2.00 / $8.00.

Sources: OpenRouter models search · AI21 pricing
